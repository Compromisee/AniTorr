Manga export formats

  • CBZ — opens in any comic reader
  • ZIP — plain image archive
  • EPUB — Apple Books / Calibre / KOReader compatible. Pre-paginated, RTL spread by default, includes dc:identifier, calibre:series, ibooks:specified-fonts.
  • PDF — one image per page, sized to original
